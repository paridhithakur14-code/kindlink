URGENT RESPONSE

Assam Flood
Relief

Communities affected by severe flooding need immediate access to food, clean water, medicines and essential supplies.

📍 Assam, India
THE SITUATION

What is happening?

Heavy rainfall has caused severe flooding in several communities. Families have been forced to leave their homes and are currently relying on temporary relief centres.

Local response organizations are working to provide essential supplies and support to affected families.

IMMEDIATE NEEDS

What is needed right now?

💧

Clean Water

Drinking water and purification supplies.

🍚

Food

Ready-to-eat meals and dry ration supplies.

💊

Medicines

Essential medicines and first-aid supplies.

🛏️

Shelter

Temporary shelter and sleeping materials.
